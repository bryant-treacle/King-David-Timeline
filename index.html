Why David Still Matters

A man after God's own heart

Not sinless, but repentant — a whole-hearted worshiper who kept returning to God after catastrophic failure.

1 Samuel 13:14 · Acts 13:22

An everlasting throne

The Davidic Covenant carries forward to the Messiah, "great David's greater Son," born in David's own Bethlehem.

Matthew 1:1 · Luke 1:32–33 · Revelation 22:16

The voice of the Psalter

Roughly seventy-three psalms bear his name, giving the church its deepest vocabulary of praise, lament, and trust.
